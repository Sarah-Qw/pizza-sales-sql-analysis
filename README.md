# Pizza Sales Analysis - SQL & Power BI

## Overview 
An end-to-end analysis of a pizza restaurant's sales data, answering 20 business questions using SQL and visualizing the key findings in Power BI.

## Business Questions Addressed
- What are the peak revenue hours and days?
- Which pizza categories and sizes generate the most revenue?
- What are the best and worst-selling pizzas?
- What are the daily and monthly ordering trends?
- What is the average order value and total revenue?

## Tools & Technologies
- SQL: Querying and answering 20 business questions.
- Power BI: Visualizing key results exported as SQL views.

## Approach
1. Data Exploration (SQL)
- Explored the dataset structure, identified key tables and relationships.
- Wrote 20 SQL queries covering revenue analysis, product performance, time-based trends, and customer ordering patterns.

2. Query Optimization
- Created SQL views for the most critical results to streamline the export process into Power BI.
- Focused on writing clean, readable queries that could be handed off to any team member.

3. Visualization (Power BI)
- Imported SQL views directly into Power BI.
- Designed a dashboard highlighting revenue trends, category performance, and peak ordering windows.
- Kept the dashboard lightweight by pre-aggregating data in SQL rather than loading raw tables.

## Key Insights
- Most orders happen around lunch (12–1 PM) and dinner (5–7 PM), which helps with staffing planning.
- Large pizzas bring in the most revenue even though they are not the most ordered size.
- A small number of pizza types make up most of the total revenue, which means the menu could be simplified.
- Weekends are busier than weekdays, opening the door for weekend-only promotions.
- The Classic category leads in both total orders and revenue.
- The Veggie category showed the lowest revenue contribution despite moderate order volume, indicating weaker profitability compared to other categories.
